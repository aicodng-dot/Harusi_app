## Release acceptance

1. A new customer registers/signs in, taps Harusi, enters names/date-time/venue, taps **Tengeneza kadi**, sees the real saved invitation, optionally edits text/colors, taps **Tuma kwa wageni**, adds/imports/selects guests, confirms the message, sends through configured Meta and sees honest progress and RSVP confirmations. No platform staff intervention or design-layer knowledge is required.
2. A guest opens only their personal link, sees the invitation/date/location, answers **Ndiyo, nitakuja** or **Siwezi**, gets persisted confirmation and views the authorized existing ticket with their name, allowance and optional table. No Kadify account is required; replay cannot double-count or undo a newer response.
3. A scanner reads that same QR, resolves the correct event/guest, enforces remaining allowance, records admission, updates actual attendance, refuses over-admission and offers Scan Next. Preserve manual code/phone lookup and existing web behavior; Android/offline parity requires real integration evidence.
4. Run the entire sequence in Kiswahili and English at all nine widths. Check keyboard-open forms, no hidden Save/RSVP controls, no clipped dialog/sidebar/header, readable preview/QR, accessible progress, loading/errors, retry and Back. Screenshots from another product are UX references, not evidence that Kadify passed QA.
